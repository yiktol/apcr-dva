A company is developing a Python application that submits data to an Amazon DynamoDB table. The company requires client-side encryption of specific data items and end-to-end protection for the encrypted data in transit and at rest. - B) When you configure the DynamoDB Encryption Client to use AWS KMS, the DynamoDB Encryption Client use
- C) The DynamoDB Encryption Client provides end-to-end protection for your data in transit and at rest. 
- D) You can use this method for server-side encryption at rest. This solution does not meet the requirem
- E) You can use this method for server-side encryption at rest. This solution does not meet the requirem

## Answer

**A, C**

- **A) **
  - When you configure the DynamoDB Encryption Client to use AWS KMS, the DynamoDB Encryption Client uses a KMS key that is always encrypted when the key is used outside of AWS KMS.
  - This cryptographic materials provider returns a unique encryption key and a signing key for every table item.
  - This method of encryption uses a symmetric KMS key.

- **C) **
  - The DynamoDB Encryption Client provides end-to-end protection for your data in transit and at rest.
  - You can encrypt selected items or attribute values in a table.

## Why the other options are incorrect

- **B) **
  - When you configure the DynamoDB Encryption Client to use AWS KMS, the DynamoDB Encryption Client uses a KMS key that is always encrypted when the key is used outside of AWS KMS.
  - This cryptographic materials provider returns a unique encryption key and a signing key for every table item.
  - This method of encryption would require a symmetric KMS key, not an asymmetric key.

- **D) **
  - You can use this method for server-side encryption at rest.
  - This solution does not meet the requirement for client-side encryption.

- **E) **
  - You can use this method for server-side encryption at rest.
  - This solution does not meet the requirement for client-side encryption.
